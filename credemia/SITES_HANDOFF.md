Keep all claims about alleged plagiarism unresolved absent evidence. Use "Veritas Vincet" with subtitle "Truth Shall Prevail" and credit Patrik Sundblom.

Review the Sites private preview. Save a version before public publishing. When ready and the share audience is allowed, select **Share → Anyone on the Internet → Publish**.

To attach the existing owned domain, open the Site's settings and select **Add domain**, enter **credemia.org**, and apply the DNS records that Sites shows using the domain registrar's DNS console. Do not copy generic/example DNS records: the unique records must be obtained from the actual deployed Site.

## Evidence integrity requirements

- All publication timestamps must come from original external sources or be tagged as user-supplied.
- Navier–Stokes, Euler and force/no-force results are different mathematical claims. Theorem equivalence must not be inferred from naming.
- QCDS 2025 Zenodo record, PRL September 2026 fusion framework and the earlier Lawson-Cordey-Mills literature must be compared through original texts, equations and algorithms rather than generalized concepts.
- Earlier ideas found in drawers need authentic dated artifacts and substantive overlap to support a priority claim; absence of publication does not make an idea nonexistent.
- A citation is not automatically appropriate acknowledgment; absence of a Crossref/DataCite reference is not automatically absence of citation in a paper.
- No person or institution should be described as copying, stealing or committing misconduct without documentary substantiation and fair opportunity to respond.
