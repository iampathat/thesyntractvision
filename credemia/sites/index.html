THE CREDEMIA EVIDENCE STANDARD

“Truth shall prevail” is a method, not a predetermined verdict.

01 · Condition formation

Identify precisely which theorem, algorithm, mechanism or contribution is being claimed, and the dates of the actual records.

02 · Oracle / constraint evaluation

Represent each required evidentiary condition as supported (1), contradicted (0), or unresolved (?). Evaluate source quality independently.

03 · Recursive QCDS inference

Use the canonical QCDS workbench to examine explicit competing possibilities, with provenance and unresolved states preserved.

04 · Truth alignment and source challenge

Stress-test the conclusion against original documents, counterevidence, exact mathematics and alternative independent explanations.
