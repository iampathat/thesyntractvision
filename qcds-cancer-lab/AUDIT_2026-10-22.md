**22 October 2026 · version 1 · synthetic research demonstrator**

**Theory and creation:** Patrik Sundblom  
**Research and development support:** OpenAI / ChatGPT  
**Status:** source-code review and deterministic in-browser/Node reference-model tests. This is not an independent clinical or quantum-hardware validation.

## Executive finding

The browser does perform real calculations: a ten-symbolic-locus search, local four-bit ideal Grover simulations, sixteen rotated logical projections per pass, 4→1 and 4→1 logarithmic probability fusion, an accumulated distribution, a full-sample rule check, and Parent Grover. **It does not search full human genomes, use NISQ hardware or independently establish cancer-related mechanisms.** The complete-sample check currently enumerates the 375 admissible 2–4-locus masks classically.

The default cohort comprises only **five synthetic labeled cases and five synthetic comparisons**. Variant labels L01–L10 and chromosome annotations are an illustrative logical geometry, **not sequence coordinates, sequenced patients, verified genes or cancer diagnosis.**

## Reproducible checks — synthetic model only

- **Default generator:** seed 173, structure mode, joint-presence relation, zero anchored positions, at least 3/5 cases and at most 1/5 controls.
- **Candidates:** 10 input positions, 1,024 possible binary selection masks; exactly 375 2–4-position masks checked classically. The local window universe contains C(10,4) = 210 windows.
- **Grover:** exact *classical* ideal-amplitude simulation, first tangent/peak or 0.999 threshold, bounded by 40 iterations. Parent Grover is likewise a classical simulation, not quantum-computer hardware.
- **Current default selected relation (after fixes):** indices [2,3,7,9] = L03, L04, L08, L10. This is an arbitrary simulated pattern with a label based on its rank, *not* a cancer biomarker.
- **Default exact conditional permutation control:** 22 / 252 = **8.7%** of possible regroupings of the ten synthetic profiles produce at least one candidate with contrast at least as large as the displayed candidate under the same threshold/oracle rules. This is not compelling evidence of a biological association, especially with n=5+5 and post-selection of settings.
- **Default rotation stability:** the six-pass model does not satisfy its own numerical stability criteria. The UI must not imply stable truth.
- **Ideal-vs-25%-uniform-readout-mixing sensitivity:** total variation of the ten-dimensional distribution was approximately **0.60** for the default data and settings. The selected top pattern happened to remain the same; the large distribution shift means that this is *not* a demonstrated noise-resistant result.
- **Null dataset example:** seed 81 with the same joint-presence settings yields no qualified relation. Empty results remain empty and are not reported as truth-aligned successes.

## What the funnel preserves — and what it does not

Each local Grover room returns a sixteen-element probability vector. Four local factors are embedded into the 1,024-state candidate representation using a normalized log-probability pool; four such group distributions are pooled again. Different mapped four-locus windows are rotated across passes, and their output distributions are bound with equal log weights.

These are **real mathematical operations on probability distributions**, not decorative animation. Their outputs can affect the rank of the qualifying combination. However, this procedure does **not** guarantee that correlations requiring five or more jointly measured loci, omitted from the local four-dimensional views, can be inferred or reconstructed. The final 375-mask classical rule check must not be mistaken for an oracle-free discovery of unconstrained higher-order relations.

Each lane is **scheduled sequentially in the browser's CPU**, so the parallel geometry represents an architectural decomposition only. It does not demonstrate quantum parallelism, an unlimited logical state space, unbounded intelligence, NISQ fault tolerance, or a quantum advantage. Each of those requires a separate experimental demonstration with resource accounting and an appropriate baseline.

## What remains unverified

1. Whole-genome ingestion for multiple genuine individuals, including indexing, encoding and audited provenance; currently only 100 invented 0/1 observations exist.
2. Biologically meaningful association/causal predicates, specified prior to evaluation; present oracles are generic joint presence and odd parity only.
3. Out-of-sample replication, patient consent, proper cancer subtype/phenotype definition and external medical validation.
4. Optimized classical baselines on the exact same biological problem and measured runtime, memory, number of queries and oracle cost.
5. An actual NISQ executable with qubit counts, coupling/layout, input-state preparation, coherence/readout budgets, device-appropriate noise profiles, shots and empirical performance.
6. Proof or falsification of cross-layer correlation retention, rotation-related information gain and scaling complexity beyond the current ten-symbolic-locus system.
7. Bias analysis and reliable false-discovery calibration on larger, blinded cohorts; label shuffling alone is not an independence or causality guarantee.
