NISQ / QUANTUM LAYER

The quantum part is not “the answer”. It is the substrate for manipulating the possibility field.

The current build emulates uniform amplitude over compatible logical states, phase marking from the active oracle set, Grover-style diffusion/amplitude amplification, logical-to-physical rotation, per-qubit noise and finite-shot measurement. It is explicitly an emulator—not a claim of current hardware advantage.

CANCER APPLICATION

Search for the structure that makes the disease state possible—not just a label or a treatment list.

Input

Genomic, pathway, phenotype, immune, microenvironment, response and resistance evidence—bound where known, open where unresolved.

Oracle factory

Top-down mechanistic hypotheses become masks and constraints. An LLM may help propose or translate them; provenance stays visible.

Dimension discovery

Rotation and exclusion measure how much each dimension changes convergence. Fragile conclusions should collapse when the wrong support is removed.

Recursive mechanism search

What survives the funnel becomes new logic for the next round, allowing the hypothesis space to deepen rather than restart from zero.

Research only. This build is not a diagnostic or treatment system and is not for clinical decision-making. The objective is to test the QCDS inference architecture against increasingly real biological data.
