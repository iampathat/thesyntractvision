CROSS-GENOME CONDITION SEARCH · SYNTHETIC RESEARCH EXAMPLE

Five genomes. Ten distant loci. Unknown relationships.

A genomic relationship need not be a contiguous DNA string. This research model generates five synthetic cancer-labeled profiles and five comparisons without inserting a predetermined winning combination. It explores previously unspecified connections across distant loci; the strongest eligible pattern, if any, emerges from the specified cohort oracle and two Grover passes.

Exactly what was computed: ten symbolic locus dimensions, 1,024 possible subset states, and candidate relationships of two to four loci. Generic case/control constraints form the first oracle; a predefined maximal-contrast refinement is the second. Both use the existing ideal Grover kernel, first-peak/tangent stopping, 0.999 threshold and maximum 40 iterations. Synthetic cohorts are generated from seed-dependent probabilities rather than an inserted answer signature. Noise, leave-one-out and label-swap tests examine internal sensitivity; none validates a causal cancer relationship.

What this does not prove: the app does not load five actual three-billion-base genomes, represent all real biological relationships, run 1,024 hardware-parallel quantum states, evaluate all combinations across billions of positions, or establish that classical computers cannot solve the task. Loading genomes into a quantum representation, building defensible gene-aware oracles, measurement/readout and independent validation remain open engineering and scientific work.

Condition

8 symbolic bits. Hard 0/1 constraints retained after exclusion rebinding; ? leaves a dimension open.

Oracle bank

Five editable positional masks evaluated as alternative predicates (OR). Biology labels are NOT encoded gene-specific rules.

Rotation + ablation

16 balanced rotations × 8 true-null families = 128 local seven-bit logical searches per pass.

Local inference

Bounded ideal Grover up to 40 steps; iteration count depends on the actual number of eligible and marked states.

Syntract bind

Full-distribution logarithmic aggregation: 128 local distributions → 8 families → 1 parent distribution.

Recursive checks

Parent Grover, feedback, and numerical convergence signals. Internal stability is not clinical validation.

Computations are browser-based idealized quantum-emulation kernels, not measured noisy quantum hardware. The current 128 logical searches are executed sequentially in the browser. No patient-specific inference, genomic annotation, outcome prediction or independent validation is implemented.
