QCDS · ROTATING PROBABILITY FUNNEL

How 16 small searches become one larger result

Imagine 16 teams each checking four DNA positions. Every team returns a whole set of probabilities, not just a single guess. Groups of four are combined, then those four groups are combined again. Rotating the positions lets the model test fresh perspectives. These are real calculations in a classical simulation, not a quantum computer running in parallel.

Technical checks: what is preserved, lost and verified?

Research scope: This browser simulates ideal Grover numerically on a classical processor, rotates sixteen four-bit windows into a 4 → 1 probability funnel, and classically checks all 375 eligible combinations against the same synthetic sample. That final check is consistency testing, not independent biological validation. The human genome, real cancer outcomes, true NISQ device noise and quantum speedup are not demonstrated. ORIGINAL 8-BIT MODEL / FORMAL METHODSInspect how the separate eight-bit laboratory works＋
Condition

8 symbolic bits. Hard 0/1 constraints retained after exclusion rebinding; ? leaves a dimension open.

Oracle bank

Five editable positional masks evaluated as alternative predicates (OR). Biology labels are NOT encoded gene-specific rules.

Rotation + ablation

16 balanced rotations × 8 true-null families = 128 local seven-bit logical searches per pass.

Local inference

Bounded ideal Grover up to 40 steps; iteration count depends on the actual number of eligible and marked states.

Syntract bind

Full-distribution logarithmic aggregation: 128 local distributions → 8 families → 1 parent distribution.

Recursive checks

Parent Grover, feedback, and numerical convergence signals. Internal stability is not clinical validation.

Computations are browser-based idealized quantum-emulation kernels, not measured noisy quantum hardware. The current 128 logical searches are executed sequentially in the browser. No patient-specific inference, genomic annotation, outcome prediction or independent validation is implemented.
