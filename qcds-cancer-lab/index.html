NISQ / QUANTUM LAYER

The quantum part is not “the answer”. It is the substrate for manipulating the possibility field.

The browser build runs the ideal bounded amplitude-amplification kernel and balanced semantic/position rotations. It does not inject invented device-calibration noise. Real NISQ noise belongs in a separate substrate layer driven by an actual hardware/noise model, while the QCDS logical semantics stay unchanged.

CANCER APPLICATION

Search for the structure that makes the disease state possible—not just a label or a treatment list.

Input

Genomic, pathway, phenotype, immune, microenvironment, response and resistance evidence—bound where known, open where unresolved.

Oracle factory

Top-down mechanistic hypotheses become masks and constraints. An LLM may help propose or translate them; provenance stays visible.

Dimension discovery

Rotation and exclusion measure how much each dimension changes convergence. Fragile conclusions should collapse when support is removed.

Recursive mechanism search

What survives the funnel becomes new logic for the next round, allowing the hypothesis space to deepen rather than restart from zero.
