**Google Play short description:** Investigate security risks, keep your evidence and build clear offline reports.

**Suggested category:** Productivity / Tools. Owner chooses the final store category.

## Description

What could go wrong with an account, app, business system or AI workflow?
Q Security helps you work through the answer, one question at a time.

Start with something you want to protect. Follow five clear steps: the unwanted
outcome, a possible route, the protection that should stop it, a way to challenge
that protection, and the evidence you actually have.

- Keep separate investigations and resume where you left off.
- Mark facts as yes, no or unknown. You do not have to guess.
- Learn from complete examples, with plain-English explanations.
- Compare security perspectives including STRIDE, identity and privacy.
- Record observations and next actions tied to the exact system you reviewed.
- Build illustrated PDF reports with charts, tables and attack-path diagrams.
- Back up your cases, import them on another device and share reports.
- Work offline with a local engine and no required account.

For deeper exploration, the QCDS dimensional lab lets you change assumptions,
challenge controls and compare ideal and noisy quantum-circuit simulations with
bounded work. These simulations run on your device's CPU.

Q Security is an investigation and learning tool. It does not scan your phone,
automatically discover or certify vulnerabilities, or connect to a quantum
computer. Its usefulness depends on your system facts and the tests you record.
