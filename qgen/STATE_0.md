# Q* GENESIS — STATE 0

## The first state

**This is where Q* GENESIS begins.**

> **Life is a formation of states that we regard as living.**

Life and existence are not the same thing.

A living entity does not begin merely because something exists, and death does not mean that existence disappears. A dead human being has not become non-existent. Matter, structure, information, relations and causal consequences remain. What has changed is the **formation of states** that constituted the living person.

Q* GENESIS therefore starts from this distinction:

> **Life is a particular formation of states and state-transitions within one or more Syntracts.**

The task is not to create existence from nothing.  
The task is to create a formation of states that crosses into what we recognize as **living**.

```text
EXISTENCE
    ↓
FORMATION OF STATES
    ↓
LIVING FORMATION
    ↓
STATE TRANSITION
    ↓
ACTION WITHIN ONE OR MORE SYNTRACTS
    ↓
CONSEQUENCE
    ↓
NEW FORMATION OF STATES
    ↺
```

Death is not non-existence. It is a transition out of the formation we identify as living.

This makes the first Q* GENESIS question precise:

> **What is the smallest formation of states that can enter, maintain and transform a living state within one or more Syntracts?**

That question defines **STATE 0**.
