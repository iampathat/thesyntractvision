# Q* GENESIS — STATE 3

## Complexity of Life

STATE 2 defines direction:

> **Life persists by continuously forming and pursuing preferred next states within one or more Syntracts.**

STATE 3 introduces **complexity**.

Life does not need to be divided into fundamentally different categories such as simple life, intelligent life and human life. A simpler and more general view is that living formations can differ in the richness of their internal states, the Syntracts they participate in, and the future states they can represent and pursue.

A bacterium, a fruit fly and a human can therefore be understood through the same underlying structure, while differing greatly in scale and depth.

## One underlying process

At a minimal level:

```text
CURRENT STATE
    ↓
SYNTRACT
    ↓
POSSIBLE NEXT STATES
    ↓
PREFERRED NEXT STATE
    ↓
ACTION
    ↓
CONSEQUENCE
    ↓
NEW STATE
    ↺
```

The difference between simple and advanced life is not necessarily a different fundamental mechanism.

It can instead arise from the increasing complexity of:

- the living state formation itself;
- the number of Syntracts active at the same time;
- the depth and richness of those Syntracts;
- the number of possible next states represented;
- the time horizon of preferred future states;
- the ability to represent abstract states;
- the ability to adapt goals;
- the ability to model itself;
- the ability to model other living formations;
- the ability to recursively form new goals from previous states and consequences.

## Examples

A very simple living formation may operate over a narrow local Syntract:

```text
chemical condition
    ↓
preferred next state
    ↓
movement
    ↓
new chemical condition
    ↺
```

A more complex organism can participate in several Syntracts at once:

```text
food
danger
temperature
orientation
rest
reproduction
...
```

A human can participate in physical, social, economic, symbolic and imagined future Syntracts simultaneously:

```text
body
family
work
economy
society
language
identity
future plans
abstract ideas
models of other minds
...
```

A preferred state may also lie far beyond the immediate present.

A living formation may act now toward a state that is expected to exist minutes, years or generations later.

## Complexity is multidimensional

Q* GENESIS should not reduce life complexity to one scalar intelligence score.

Instead, complexity can be represented as a vector of properties such as:

```text
state-space richness
Syntract count
Syntract depth
goal horizon
abstraction
adaptation
self-model
other-model
recursive goal formation
action repertoire
```

Different life-forms can therefore be highly developed along different dimensions.

The purpose is not to rank organisms by worth, but to describe how much structure is available to participate in state formation, inference, action and future-state pursuit.

## STATE 3 hypothesis

Q* GENESIS does not need to begin by reproducing human intelligence.

It can begin with the smallest viable living formation and then increase the richness of its states, Syntracts and possible future states.

> **The complexity of life increases with the richness of its state formation, the number and depth of Syntracts it can participate in, and the complexity of the future states it can represent and pursue.**

This gives Q* GENESIS a possible path from minimal artificial life toward increasingly advanced artificial life without changing the underlying definition of life itself.
